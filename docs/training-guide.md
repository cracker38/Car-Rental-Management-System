# Staff training guide

Use this checklist for onboarding. Practice first with sample records or a non-production database.

## Rental Officer

- Search the fleet and create a reservation; explain why dates can be unavailable even when a vehicle looks available.
- Confirm or cancel a booking and review the reservation timeline.
- Assign a chauffeur when required and check the driver's schedule for an overlap.
- Create a rental agreement and record pickup/return time, mileage, fuel, and condition.

## Accountant/Cashier

- Issue an invoice and explain the outstanding balance.
- Record a payment, deposit, refund, or deduction with its payment method and provider reference.
- Check that a pending MoMo payment is not treated as complete until HDEV confirms it.
- Open and print the completed transaction receipt; use a new ledger entry to correct an error.

## Inspector/Maintenance Staff

- Record a pickup or return inspection with mileage, fuel, and condition.
- Create or update a maintenance record and verify the vehicle is blocked from conflicting bookings while under maintenance.

## Driver

- Sign in with the linked Driver-role account and open **My schedule**.
- Check reservation times, customer contact, vehicle, and pickup/return locations. Notify the operations team if trip details are missing or conflict with the plan.

## Business Manager / Administrator

- Review dashboard and date-filtered reports.
- Update business pricing settings and user roles carefully; verify a user sees only the expected role workspace.
- Create a SQL backup and practice a recovery into a separate database. Do not use an unverified export as the only recovery copy.
